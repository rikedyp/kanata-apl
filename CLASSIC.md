# Kanata layout for Dyalog Classic users.
This layout contains the en-GB English layer with an APL shifting key, underscored alphabet mode.

By default, the APL keys are <kbd>Caps Lock</kbd> and <kbd>Right Ctrl</kbd>. These can be changed in the section beginning `deflayermap base`.

The backtick key (top-left), while held, allows the production of underscored letters from the alphabet keys. Pressed once and released (tapped), it produces a backtick like normal.

Certain key combinations produce special control codes that are recognised by the Dyalog interpreter to perform certain actions. This has been tested in PowerShell and PuTTY.

|Action|Shortcut            |
|------|--------------------|
|ED    |Shift+Enter         |
|TC    |Ctrl+Enter          |
|FD    |Ctrl+Shift+Enter    |
|BK    |Ctrl+Shift+Backspace|
|EP    |Esc                 |
|QT    |Shift+Esc           |
|FX    |Ctrl+Shift+S        |
|CP    |Ctrl+Shift+C        |
|CT    |Ctrl+Shift+X        |
|PT    |Ctrl+Shift+V        |
|SA    |Ctrl+Shift+A        |
|TB    |Ctrl+Shift+Tab      |
